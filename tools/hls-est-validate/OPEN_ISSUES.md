## O1. Loop-carried memory dependences never reach the modulo schedulers (filed 2026-10-09, review of 99ac05534, finding D1)
- **Symptom:** `a[i] = a[i-1] * k` (f32) schedules at II = 1 with both the simplex scheduler (`--convert-affine-to-loopschedule`) and the greedy one (`=fast-threshold=0`); the model's own recurrence bound is 6 (load 1 + mulf 4 + store 1). Only recurrences through `iter_args` are honoured (test/hls-est/Passes/fast_schedule_recurrence.mlir). The XFAIL lit test test/hls-est/Passes/memory_recurrence_xfail.mlir encodes the expected II and starts failing-as-XPASS when this is fixed.
- **Cause (pre-existing, inherited from upstream CIRCT's AffineToLoopSchedule):** `lib/Dialect/Resource/Passes/AffineToLoopScheduleModified.cpp` `runOnOperation` copies the analysis (`auto dependenceAnalysis = getAnalysis<MemoryDependenceAnalysis>();`) and `lowerAffineStructures` calls `replaceOp` on that copy when it lowers `affine.load`/`affine.store`/`affine.store_enable` to `memref.load`/`memref.store`/`hls.store_enable`. `lib/Dialect/Resource/Passes/CyclicSchedulingAnalysisModified.cpp` then reads the AnalysisManager's cached instance, whose keys are still the erased affine ops, so `getDependences(<lowered op>)` is always empty. The `replaceOp()` re-keying of 99ac05534 is correct but inert, and the grouped/memoized dependence analysis of that commit computes results nothing consumes (172 s of the ~390 s on forgebench/diff_orders_module).
- **What activating it costs (reviewer's experiment, reverted):** with `auto &dependenceAnalysis` the recurrences arrive on both schedulers (II 2 / 6 / 2 on `a[i] = a[i-1] + k` i32, the f32 case above, and its distance-3 variant), but 16 of the 42 test/hls-est/Estimation inputs then fail: "problem is infeasible" (polybench/atax, machsuite/nw, ...) and "multiple sinks detected" (basic/arraypartition_block, ...), because the intra-iteration (distance-0, including WAR/WAW) edges and the sink anchoring had never been exercised with real memory dependences. Canonical totals of polybench/atax, gesummv and polybench_pragmas/syr2k stayed 67 / 134 / 56 in that experiment.
- **Why it matters for Vitis matching:** a loop-carried memory dependence raises II, which lowers the per-bank port demand and can change the chosen RAM type and the x16/x32 aspect ratio. Activation is real work (dependence classes, anchoring) and needs its own totals check against the Vitis ground truth before it is adopted.
